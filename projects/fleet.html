How it works

Fleet v1.9.0 runs directly inside Antigravity's auxiliary pane (SIDECAR_UI_ENTRYPOINT_AUX_PANE, installed atomically to ~/.gemini/config/plugins/fleet/) backed by a zero-dependency Node ESM sidecar (304.1 KiB raw / 91.9 KiB (94,127 B) pre-gzipped -1.3%, 77.66 ms cold boot -5.7%, 71.06 MB --jitless peak RSS -2.3% / -66.5% vs baseline, 1,461.2 req/s +5.4% with 21.48 ms max tail -12.9%). The legacy standalone Rust server remains preserved at tag v0.12.0.

01 · Swarm Bottleneck Heat Bar & Copy Blocker

Spot & escalate stalls in 1 click

Proportional working / waiting / blocked heat bar inside Needs You highlights swarm bottlenecks with a clickable Top #seq jump and 1-click Copy blocker generating ~/-masked Markdown (0 raw /home/ paths).

02 · 91.9 KiB Gzip & 71.06 MB --jitless RSS

-66.5% RSS, 77.7ms boot & 21.5ms max tail

Consolidated SVG icons + shared helpers shrink gzip to 91.9 KiB, while createMentionsMatcher, SHARED_CHUNK_BUF, TAIL_CAP = 16, and parseQueryFast lift 16w HTTP to 1,461.2 req/s (21.48 ms max).

03 · Worktree Diff Peek & 28/28 Cold Fuzz

CWE-22/59 confinement & zero leaks

Bounded GET /api/worktrees/diff hunks confined via realpathSync, clean ~/workspace/app(-wt) captures, zlib.crc32 ETags, and 28/28 raw-TCP fuzz security checks pass.
